# CleanPro Portfolio Website

This is a professional cleaning service website consisting of multiple pages designed to showcase services, company information, and contact details.

## Common Elements Across All Pages
- **Header/Navigation:** A sticky top bar containing the company logo ("CleanPro"), navigation links (Home, Services, About Us, Contact), and a "Get Free Quote" button. The "Portfolio" link has been completely removed to streamline the user experience.
- **Footer:** A comprehensive footer containing company info, quick navigation links (matching the header), contact details (Address, Phone, Email), and social media icons.

## Pages Overview

### 1. Home (`index.html`)
The main landing page of the website. It displays:
- **Hero Section:** A visually appealing header with an image background and a call to action.
- **Trust Indicators:** Badges highlighting licenses, insurance, and expert staff.
- **Why Choose Us:** Key benefits of choosing the service (Reliable, Eco-Friendly, Quality Assurance).
- **Services Overview:** A quick glance at the primary services offered (Commercial, Office, Residential, Real Estate, Industrial, Janitorial).
- **Industries We Serve:** Image cards showing the types of facilities serviced (Corporate Offices, Auto Dealerships, Property Management).
- **Process Section:** A step-by-step guide on how the cleaning service works (Inspection, Quote, Cleaning Plan, Quality Check).
- **Testimonials:** Reviews from past clients.
- **Call to Action (CTA):** A final section encouraging visitors to schedule an inspection.

### 2. Services (`services.html`)
A dedicated page detailing the cleaning services offered. It provides in-depth information on specific cleaning packages and industry-specific solutions.

### 3. About Us (`about.html`)
This page provides background information about the CleanPro company. It displays:
- The company's story and history.
- Mission statement and core values.
- Information about the team and training standards.

### 4. Contact (`contact.html`)
The page where users can reach out to the company. It displays:
- Contact information (Phone, Email, Address).
- A contact form for direct inquiries.
- A location map and hours of operation.
